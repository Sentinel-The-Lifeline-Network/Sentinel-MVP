The Problem

In emergencies, every second matters.

People in danger may not have time to call, type, explain, or search for help. Traditional emergency systems create delays that cost lives.

No time to explain

When panic hits, forming words is hard. Traditional calls require you to speak, wait, and describe — Sentinel needs only one tap.

Unfamiliar locations

You may not know your address in an emergency. Sentinel automatically captures and shares your GPS coordinates without any manual input.

Poor network conditions

Emergency alerts are queued locally and synced the moment connection returns, so network issues don't prevent help from being requested.

Delayed response

Minutes lost in traditional dispatch can be the difference between life and death. Sentinel notifies trusted contacts the instant SOS is triggered.

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Sentinel is a mobile-first emergency response platform. It allows anyone to trigger an instant SOS alert with one tap, automatically capture and share their live GPS location, and notify trusted emergency contacts through SMS and email.

When you press the large SOS button, Sentinel immediately captures your GPS location, creates an emergency alert, and sends messages containing your location and a live tracking link to all your emergency contacts. The entire process happens automatically without any additional input from you.

Emergency alerts are queued locally on your device and automatically dispatched as soon as connectivity is available. Sentinel is built for anyone in a community that values safety. At the individual level, it's for students, professionals, women traveling alone, elderly individuals, and anyone who wants a faster way to call for help. At the community level, it's built for universities, NYSC camps, residential estates, transportation hubs, and any organization that wants to build a structured emergency response capability for the people they serve.

Sentinel is currently a Progressive Web App (PWA), so it runs in any modern browser and can be added to a phone home screen without an app store download. A dedicated mobile app is planned soon, while the PWA keeps Sentinel easy to access and deploy today.
